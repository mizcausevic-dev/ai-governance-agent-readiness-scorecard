# Kinetic Gain Embedded

This repo demonstrates the executive-intelligence shape of Kinetic Gain Embedded:

- ingest an AI governance and agent-readiness packet
- analyze exposure, savings posture, investment priority, and board-pack freshness
- convert the result into an executive scorecard, board brief, and diligence-ready evidence layer

For embedded product work, the same primitive becomes an in-app board and investor operating surface where leaders need a clean risk, cost, and investment narrative without direct access to raw platform backends.
